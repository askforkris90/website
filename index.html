✍️ About the Author

🖊️

AskForKris90

👋 I'm an independent author and self-publisher at langysitemedia.io. I write stories that capture the feel of real eras — not just dates and events, but the texture of life in each generation. The music, the money worries, the technology, the relationships. Every book I write is anchored in a specific generational experience while reaching for something universal. 📚

All my books are published free of charge directly from GitHub. No gatekeepers, no publishing houses, no barriers. If a story resonates with you, share it. That's all I ask. 🙏

💡 Recommended read for first-timers: Start with 3001 if you're a Millennial, or Hello, Friend if the analogue era calls to you. Gen Z readers — ZKash Air is waiting. 🚀
